Euan McGill

I am a linguist and PhD researcher in Natural Language Processing at Universitat Pompeu Fabra, Barcelona. I work on the EU Horizon 2020 project SignON, which aims to develop a spoken-to-sign language translation platform accessible to all. My academic research centres on accessibility in NLP, including Automatic Text Simplification, Corpus Linguistics, Machine Translation, and data augmentation methods. I am also passionate about democracy and the political geography of Britain.
